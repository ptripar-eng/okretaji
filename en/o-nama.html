About the Okretaji portal

Okretaji is an independent project covering cars, motorcycles, motorsport, technology, and energy. The goal is to offer readers a concise overview of topics and links to original information.

How we work

News may be gathered from publicly available RSS feeds and summarised using automated tools. An automated summary may contain errors; please open the original article for important details. We do not publish full texts from other publishers.

The energy section strives to distinguish between measured data, estimates, and opinions, citing methodology and sources whenever available.
