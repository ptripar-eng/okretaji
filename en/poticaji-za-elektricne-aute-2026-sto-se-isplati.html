What the public call sets out

Public call EnU-2/26 of the Environmental Protection and Energy Efficiency Fund was published on 9 October 2026. After postponements, applications are scheduled to open on Thursday 5 November at 9:00 via the eFZOEU system. The grant for electric passenger cars is up to 40% of the eligible price, subject to fixed caps. Before applying, check the latest amendments and documents on the Fund's official website.

Eligible are adult natural persons with residence in Croatia, for one eligible vehicle. Only battery-electric vehicles are eligible; electric bicycles, including mopeds, are not covered. The vehicle must be new, and eligible purchase methods are cash or credit, as well as financial leasing according to the call's terms. Operating lease is not eligible. The subsidy is paid out after the purchase, registration and submission of appropriate evidence. Therefore, financing for the purchase must be secured in advance, with special rules for financial leasing described below.

Important: Once submitted, an application cannot be changed or supplemented, and the same applicant cannot submit a new one. Applications are processed in the order received. Prepare the required documents before applications open. Final eligibility depends on the Fund's conditions and the individual application.

Deadlines and buyer obligations

A vehicle paid in full before the call was published on 9 October 2026 is not eligible. A deposit is eligible if it was incurred and paid no earlier than 1 January 2026; paying a deposit is not required to apply. The vehicle must be bought and registered by 31 March 2028, and the complete payment request submitted by 30 April 2028. The vehicle must be kept in ownership or use for two years from the date the Fund pays out.

A financial leasing agreement can have a repayment period of a maximum of five years. Before submitting a disbursement request, the beneficiary must settle the down payment and instalments in a total amount equal to or greater than the approved subsidy, and must settle all remaining obligations from the agreement and provide proof to the Fund no later than five years from the date of the subsidy disbursement (public call, pp. 3 and 7). It is necessary to secure financing for the total purchase, but leasing does not require paying the full price before the subsidy is disbursed.

Why does the €35,000 threshold matter so much?

Cheaper cars do not automatically get €9,000

For a car costing €16,984, 40% is €6,793.60. Only when the price reaches €22,500 does 40% equal the full €9,000. This difference matters for the smallest electric city cars.

One euro more can mean €2,000 less support

An electric car costing exactly €35,000 comes to €26,000 with the full grant. A car costing €35,001 falls into the band with a maximum of €7,000 and costs €28,001 after the full grant. That is why the final offer should be checked for the price including VAT, extra equipment and any discounts.

How to apply?

  1. Check that you can access the eFZOEU system via fondovi.gov.hr with an NIAS credential of substantial assurance level.
  2. Obtain an indicative offer with the price in euros, manufacturer, model, powertrain type and vehicle type-approval category. Fill in any missing details with a catalogue, certificate or equivalent document.
  3. Prepare the application form from Annex 1 in XLSX format, a certificate of residence no older than 30 days on the day of application and the signed statement from Annex 2. Documents other than the application form are submitted in PDF format.
  4. If the application is submitted by a representative, attach a special power of attorney under Annex 3, certified by a notary public. A power of attorney issued to a lawyer does not need notarisation.
  5. Applications open on 5 November 2026 at 9:00. The call runs until the end of 2026 or until the funds are used up, whichever comes first. Check all details and attachments before submitting.
  6. Track the status in the “Moje prijave” (My applications) module. Submitting an application does not guarantee a grant. Before committing to a seller, check the Fund's decision, contract and conditions.

Can you buy a car in Slovenia or Germany with the Croatian grant?

Yes, buying in another EU country is eligible according to the Fund's clarification. The vehicle must meet the call's definition of a new vehicle, be type-approved for road use in Croatia and, after purchase, be registered for the first time in Croatia. A previous regular registration in another country is not eligible. The Fund does not regard trade, export (for example Zoll) or transferable plates as registration for the purposes of the call. Documents in a foreign language require a translation into Croatian certified by a sworn court interpreter. Source: FZOEU, answer 40.

When buying a new vehicle from the EU, the application states the vehicle's value including Croatian VAT. An advertised foreign price should therefore not automatically be compared with the Croatian price on the invoice. Ask for an offer that clearly separates the vehicle price, VAT and additional costs. FZOEU, answer 10.

Delivery and type approval are not an extra grant. Costs of delivery, preparation, type approval, issuing the COC, documentation and other related costs that are not part of the vehicle price are not included in the co-financing base. Compare the total cost of getting the car on the road, including translation and transport, and ask the seller for written confirmation of the registration history. FZOEU, answer 38.

What if the car has been used for test drives?

Mileage from test drives on transferable plates does not by itself rule out the grant. The Fund sets no maximum mileage for such vehicles, but they must meet the definition of a new vehicle and all other conditions of the call. A “demo” label in an advert is not sufficient proof of eligibility. FZOEU, answer 36.

Take a child seat and pushchair to the test drive. Check the space behind the driver, ISOFIX access, the width and shape of the boot and comfort on poor roads. For trips, ask for the DC charging time from 10 to 80% and the conditions of that measurement, and for home charging, the supported AC power. It also matters who services the car, how far away the workshop is and whether a courtesy car is available.

Equipment can change the whole calculation. A car costing €34,500 comes to €25,500 after the full grant. If you pay €1,000 extra for equipment included in the vehicle price, the price rises to €35,500, the grant drops to €7,000 and the cost after the grant rises to €28,500. In this example, a €1,000 extra raises the final cost by €3,000.

How much do you save with home charging, and how much at public chargers?

The grant reduces the purchase price, while the price of charging determines part of the later costs. Seven things to check before paying a deposit

  1. Written offer: exact version and battery, price including VAT, equipment, discount conditions and separate fees.
  2. Deposit and delivery: agree in writing what happens if you do not get the grant or delivery is late. A refund of the deposit does not follow automatically from the Fund's rules; it depends on your contract with the seller.
  3. Real-world space: try the child seats, pushchair, luggage and access to the rear bench.
  4. Charging on your routes: check your home connection, the car's AC charging, available public chargers, their prices and an alternative if a charger is occupied.
  5. Winter journey: look for a comparable test at the stated temperature and speed, including heating and a battery reserve. The WLTP figure is not a motorway range promise.
  6. Battery and warranty: ask for the written warranty terms, mileage limit and any guaranteed remaining capacity. The battery chemistry label alone is not a sufficient criterion for choosing a vehicle.
  7. Software and service: check the software version, update method, operation of the air conditioning, navigation and phone connectivity, as well as the availability of service and parts.

Conclusion

Incentives significantly lower the upfront price of an electric car, but they do not change all the costs of ownership. Before buying, compare your real range needs, charging options and total costs. The €35,000 threshold deserves special attention, because a small change in the final price can change the amount of the grant.
