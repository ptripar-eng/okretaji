        In numbers

        Traffic, emissions and electric vehicles – unbiased facts.

        Greenhouse gas emissions, 2024.

        Who is polluting the world?

        In 2024, the world emitted a record 53.2 billion tonnes of CO₂e (+1.3 %). The EU is one of the few major economies to have reduced emissions (−1.8 %) and is now 35 % below 1990 levels.

        Source: EDGAR / JRC, 2025.

        New cars in the EU, 2025.

        What are Europeans buying?

        Petrol and diesel combined fell to 35.5 % of the market, down from 45.2 % the previous year.

        Source: ACEA, 2026.

        Transport and climate in the EU, 2023.

        How much does transport pollute?

        31 %of all EU emissions come from transport
        73 %of transport emissions come from road transport
        52 %of transport emissions come from cars and vans

        Unlike industry and energy, transport emissions are higher today than in 1990.

        Source: European Environment Agency, 2025

        How green is the energy really?

        Solar and wind farms have emissions from manufacturing, construction and disposal. A fair comparison counts emissions per kilowatt-hour generated over the whole lifetime.
