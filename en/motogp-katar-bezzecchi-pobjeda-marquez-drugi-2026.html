Bezzecchi's answer to Márquez: from Saturday crash to victory in Qatar

Marco Bezzecchi triumphed at Lusail ahead of Marco Márquez and Raúl Fernández. Only a day earlier, his weekend had looked entirely different.

Okretaji · 8 November 2026 · Editorial Office

His Saturday ended in the gravel, and his Sunday on the top step of the podium. Marco Bezzecchi won the main MotoGP race of the Qatar Grand Prix at the Lusail circuit, ahead of Ducati's Marco Márquez and Trackhouse Aprilia's Raúl Fernández.

Such an outcome gives special weight to what happened a day earlier. In the sprint, Bezzecchi and Márquez collided while battling for the lead. The Italian crashed, the Spaniard continued and won, and the stewards deemed their contact a racing incident with no penalty. Bezzecchi's response arrived on the track, in the weekend's most important race.

Aprilia won the duel, Ducati vital points

Bezzecchi's victory confirms the pace Aprilia already showed in qualifying. The Italian set a lap record of 1:50.028 back then and took pole position by 0.266 seconds ahead of Márquez. Saturday's crash did not erase that pace; Sunday's result turned it into a win.

With second place, Márquez continued to collect points in the title fight. He arrived in Qatar with the opportunity to wrap up the championship before the final two weekends, but Sunday's result did not bring him mathematical confirmation of the title.

Fernández completes the podium

Raúl Fernández's third place further marked an excellent weekend for Aprilia's machinery. Two different Aprilia setups finished on the podium, while Márquez on the factory Ducati ended up between them. This is also a message for the end of the season: Ducati holds the points advantage in the hands of its leading rider, but Aprilia does not intend to back down on the track.

The story of the weekend is not just the victory

Jorge Martín arrived in Qatar with an injured wrist, and in Saturday's sprint he managed to climb from 11th on the grid to third place. Francesco Bagnaia, meanwhile, missed the main race after fracturing bones in his foot during a Saturday incident with Fermín Aldeguer.

For Bezzecchi, Qatar remains a weekend where the hardest possible Saturday outcome was followed by the best Sunday response. For Márquez, it remains another step towards the title, but also a warning that Aprilia has an answer to Ducati's pace.
