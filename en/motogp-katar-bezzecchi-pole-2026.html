Bezzecchi smashes Lusail record and takes pole ahead of Márquez

Aprilia dominated Saturday practice, and Bezzecchi set a new lap record in qualifying.

Okretaji · 7 November 2026 · Editorial Team

Marco Bezzecchi claimed pole position for the Qatar Grand Prix at the Lusail circuit, setting a lap time of 1:50.028. The Aprilia rider was 0.266 seconds faster than Marc Márquez on the Ducati, while Fabio Di Giannantonio set the third-fastest time (+0.299 s).

Aprilia already showed exceptional pace in Saturday practice: the top four places were taken by Bezzecchi, Raúl Fernández, Ai Ogura and Jorge Martín. However, in qualifying Martín failed to repeat that speed and finished 11th, more than a second behind his teammate.

What do the results mean?

The second row of the grid consists of Raúl Fernández, Francesco Bagnaia and Ai Ogura. Bezzecchi's result is particularly significant as Márquez enters the season finale as the championship leader, with Martín as his closest challenger.

Single-lap speed is not the same as race pace. Lusail will once again place the emphasis on tyre conservation, especially during longer stints.

Tyres shake up the practice order

Aprilia opened Saturday practice on the medium rear tyre, while the leading riders from Ducati, KTM and Honda went out on the softer compound. Marc Márquez held the fastest time for a long time, but after switching to a soft rear tyre, Bezzecchi jumped from sixth to first place. Behind him came Raúl Fernández, Ai Ogura and Jorge Martín: four Aprilias at the top of the timesheets.

Unlike practice, qualifying brought a direct duel between Bezzecchi and Márquez. Márquez was the first to break Fabio Di Giannantonios previous lap record, only for Bezzecchi to better it soon after. Despite a mistake at the final corner, the Italian set a record lap of 1:50.028.

Martín unhappy, Acosta continues streak

Jorge Martín, one of Márquezs main title rivals, qualified only eleventh. After his lap, he gestured angrily towards the KTM riders, feeling they had slowed him down. Pedro Acosta, on the other hand, came through Q1 to claim eighth on the grid, continuing his streak of outqualifying his team-mate.

There are only 18 riders on the entry list in Qatar: factory Yamaha and Prima Pramac withdrew from the event due to travel restrictions linked to the regional conflict. Jake Dixon, substituting for Honda HRC, is competing in MotoGP for the first time since 2021 and qualified seventeenth.
