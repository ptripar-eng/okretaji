Privacy

Okretaji is a static website. The hosting provider may process technical data necessary for delivering the site. External fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts.

Google Analytics

With prior consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand website traffic and usage. Google Analytics is not loaded until you select „Accept analytics”. If you select „Reject”, analytics remains disabled. Your choice is saved in your browser. Clearing website data will display the consent prompt again.

Measurement ID: G-426646FTQH. Google may process data according to its privacy policy and may process it outside the European Economic Area, with appropriate safeguards applied when applicable. More information: Google Privacy Policy.

Facebook and Instagram

Okretaji publishes its own posts on its Facebook page and Instagram profile via the Meta app „automatika”. The app is used solely for publishing Okretaji content; it does not log visits, collect personal data, or read messages. Meta's privacy policy applies on Facebook and Instagram.

Contact and Data Deletion

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, write to info@okretaji.com. We respond within a month at the latest.
