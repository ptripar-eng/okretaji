Formula 1 · São Paulo GP

Verstappen fastest in final practice in Brazil, Antonelli just 25 thousandths behind

Red Bull set the fastest time at Interlagos, but engine penalty grid drops will shake up the starting order.

Okretaji · 7 November 2026 · Editorial Team

Max Verstappen finished free practice three for the São Paulo Grand Prix at the top of the timesheets. The Red Bull driver posted a 1:11.622 to edge out Kimi Antonelli by just 0.025 seconds. Lando Norris was third (+0.131 s), ahead of George Russell and Charles Leclerc.

The fastest times were set in the closing stages of the session on soft tyres, just before heavier rain arrived. The top four were separated by less than two tenths, pointing to an extremely close single-lap pace.

What do the results mean?

Verstappen still faces a back-of-the-grid start due to power unit component changes. Hamilton and Leclerc have ten-place grid penalties for internal combustion engine changes, and Russell a five-place penalty.

Lewis Hamilton reported unusual engine vibrations and smoke in the cockpit, after which he cut his session short in 12th place. Qualifying will therefore, alongside changeable weather, be crucial for assessing the actual starting order.

Verstappen's fastest lap does not mean starting from the front

Red Bull changed multiple power unit components on Verstappen's car, including the internal combustion engine, turbocharger and exhaust system. Due to a total of 30 grid penalty places, the Dutchman will start the race from the back of the grid. Ferrari handed ten-place grid penalties to Hamilton and Leclerc due to new engines, and George Russell got a five-place penalty.

Smoke in Hamilton's cockpit and trouble with Stroll's ERS

During practice, Hamilton reported unusual power unit vibrations and excessive engine braking. After briefly returning to the track, he was called back into the pits again because he noticed smoke in the cockpit and a concerning engine noise. He finished in 12th place, without a final run on soft tyres. Lance Stroll managed only seven laps before an energy recovery system (ERS) issue kept him in the garage.

First qualifying simulations only in the final stages

For most of the practice session, the drivers used medium tyres. Only in the final fifteen minutes did they switch to the softer compound and start qualifying simulations. Russell, Norris, Antonelli and Verstappen took turns at the top, and the gap between the top four at the end was just 0.184 seconds. Rain started falling right at the end of the session.

Nico Hülkenberg finally got some meaningful mileage after Audi had technical problems on Friday during young Freddie Slater's outing. Arvid Lindblad was seventh for Racing Bulls, while local driver Gabriel Bortoleto finished tenth in an Audi.
