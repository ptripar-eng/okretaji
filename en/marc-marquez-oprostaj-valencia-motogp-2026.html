Marc Márquez is leaving MotoGP, Valencia is his final race

In the Ducati garage, he announced the end of his career. He was second in the opening practice session, a hundredth behind Marco Bezzecchi.

Okretaji · 27 November 2026 · Editorial staff

On Friday in the Ducati garage in Valencia, Marc Márquez announced his retirement from MotoGP. A few minutes later, he went out for the first free practice session and set the second-fastest time. Marco Bezzecchi was 0.010 seconds faster. On Sunday, Márquez will race his final Grand Prix at the Ricardo Tormo circuit.

Last weekend in Portimão, he claimed his tenth world title, his eighth in the premier class. This puts him level with Giacomo Agostini. He won six MotoGP titles with Honda and his last two with Ducati.

Leaving two seasons before his contract expires

Márquez had a contract with Ducati until the end of 2028. According to Reuters, he decided to retire at last month's Japanese Grand Prix after the physical and mental toll of injuries and surgeries piled up.

He arrived in Valencia as champion. On Sunday at that circuit, he will line up on the MotoGP grid for the very last time with the number 93.
