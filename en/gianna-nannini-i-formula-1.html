Gianna Nannini and Formula 1?

Seemingly incompatible, yet her brother Alessandro won the Japanese Grand Prix in a race everyone remembers for Senna and Prost. When a helicopter crash cut short his Formula 1 career, he returned to racing in one of the most famous Alfas of the nineties.

Okretaji · 10 November 2026

If the surname Nannini first brings to mind the raspy voice from the song Bello e impossibile, look for it at the top of the official standings of the 1989 Japanese Grand Prix as well. Right there is Alessandro Nannini, Benetton Ford.

A race remembered for two other drivers

Suzuka that Sunday was the stage for the showdown between Ayrton Senna and Alain Prost. Their McLarens collided in the chicane, and Prost retired. Senna rejoined the race and crossed the finish line first, only to be subsequently disqualified. In the official classification, the winner was Nannini, followed by Riccardo Patrese and Thierry Boutsen.

Alessandro arrived in Formula 1 with Minardi in 1986, and then moved to Benetton. Suzuka remained his only F1 victory. The story of it usually begins with the collision of the two McLarens. Nannini's story is also interesting because of what happened afterwards.

Return to the wheel of an Alfa

In October 1990, Alessandro severely injured his right arm in a helicopter crash. He never returned to Formula 1. However, he raced and won again. When Alfa Romeo entered the German DTM with the 155 V6 Ti as a factory team in 1993, Alessandro was in the line-up alongside Nicola Larini. The DTM records 14 wins for him in the DTM and ITC from 1993 to 1996.

When Alessandro entered Gianna's frame

Gianna meanwhile had her own story. Her voice from Bello e impossibile and I maschi became recognizable beyond Italy as well. When she released the compilation Bomboloni, her brother Alessandro also appeared in the music video for the title track. If you have been looking for him only in old race footage until now, look for him there too.
